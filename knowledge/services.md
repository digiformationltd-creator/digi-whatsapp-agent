# Services & Enterprise Modules

DIGI BIZ OS provides 12 unified enterprise modules built into one desktop application:

1. **Voice Assistant (JARVIS Desktop AI):** Hands-free voice control to check figures, trigger actions, generate invoices, and operate apps (<200ms latency).
2. **Digi CRM:** Complete sales pipeline, orders, customers, deals, tasks, tickets, and automated branded PDF invoice generation.
3. **Digi WhatsApp:** 24/7 autonomous WhatsApp sales agent that answers client questions, quotes prices from your catalogue, and handles bookings.
4. **Digi AI:** Advanced AI reasoning, copywriting, document generation, and creative image generation with 20+ free fallback providers.
5. **Digi Intelligence:** In-depth B2B lead generation, competitor analysis, SEO audits, and winning e-commerce product research.
6. **Digi Studio:** Creative photo editor, video/audio cutter, natural neural TTS voice synthesis, and document/PDF OCR.
7. **Digi Flow:** Visual drag-and-drop workflow automator triggered by voice or scheduled timers.
8. **Digi Code:** Offline and online AI software development environment to build scripts and automations without cloud lock-in.
9. **Digi Marketing:** Multi-channel social media marketing scheduler for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Telegram, and YouTube.
10. **Digi Connectors:** Connects Shopify, Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Gmail, Google Workspace, Stripe, and PayPal to your CRM.
11. **Digi Models:** Seamless model switching between free online models, private API keys, and local offline Ollama LLMs.
12. **Built-in Specialists:** Library of hundreds of specialized AI workers (coder, accountant, tax specialist, copywriter, legal assistant).
